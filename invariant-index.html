ACorrectness & money handling

Duplicate-charge prevention on retry, ledger debit/credit balance, exact reversals, calculation determinism, and decision-table accuracy against a labelled corpus — six archetypes covering every pattern that touches state or money.

BMessaging & integration

Proves messages aren't dropped, duplicated, or misrouted between services — transformation fidelity, delivery guarantees with dead-letter handling, ordering under concurrency, and contract compatibility verified against the live producer, not a stale mock.

CLoad & capacity

Proves rate limits, batch cutoffs, and multi-tenant systems hold their declared limits under real concurrent load, and that one customer's traffic burst cannot degrade another's.

DResilience & deployment

Proves circuit breakers actually open, fallbacks actually degrade gracefully, and deploys/rotations complete with zero attributable failed requests — verified by injecting the real fault, not by reviewing configuration.

EData integrity

Proves reporting and analytics data converges correctly from source systems, historical records can't be silently rewritten, and reconciliation is independently recomputed rather than re-reading the same aggregate.

FSecurity

Proves access control and data protection actually work end-to-end — not merely configured correctly. The most rigorously reviewed pair in the corpus.

Risk avoidedReview caught an authorisation check that could report a pass even during a live outage, and a test design that couldn't distinguish a fully compromised device from a healthy one — both fixed before merge.

GObservability & client experience

Proves monitoring and alerting actually fire when something breaks (not just that they're configured to), and that client apps degrade gracefully offline and meet real performance budgets on real devices.
